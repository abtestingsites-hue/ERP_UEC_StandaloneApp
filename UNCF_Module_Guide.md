# UNCF Module - Presentation Guide

The **UNCF** (Universal Nature Care and Cure Foundation) is the charitable and non-profit wing of the organization. Unlike the other modules which handle healthcare operations or ambulance logistics, this module focuses strictly on managing donations, social projects, volunteers, and foundation outreach.

Here is the page-by-page explanation of the UNCF side navigation bar:

## 1. Foundation Command Center
* **What it does:** The primary dashboard for the foundation. It aggregates the total funds collected, active projects, and live campaign metrics.
* **Who uses it:** Foundation Trustees, NGO Directors, and Chief Coordinators.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is the heartbeat of your foundation. At a glance, you can see how close you are to your funding targets for various charity drives without needing to dig through spreadsheets."*

## 2. Projects
* **What it does:** A project management board for specific charitable initiatives (e.g., 'Elder Food Support', 'Medical Aid Drive'). It tracks the monetary target vs. what has actually been collected and allocated.
* **Who uses it:** Project Managers, Charity Coordinators.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"When you launch a new social initiative, you track its lifecycle here. You can see exactly how much money a specific project needs, and how much has been fulfilled by donors."*

## 3. Funding
* **What it does:** The high-level financial tracking for major capital. This tracks inbound corporate CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) grants, government funding, or large-scale institutional investments.
* **Who uses it:** Finance Team, Trustees.

## 4. Outreach
* **What it does:** The marketing and communications hub for the foundation. It tracks campaigns (like Meta Ads or WhatsApp blasts) used to attract donors or spread awareness for a project.
* **Who uses it:** Marketing Team, PR Managers.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"If you are spending money on Instagram ads to get donations for an emergency relief fund, this page tracks the leads generated and the exact return on your marketing spend."*

## 5. People
* **What it does:** The HR module specifically for the foundation. While the main 'Home' HR manages nurses and drivers, this tab manages the trustees, unpaid volunteers, and social workers.
* **Who uses it:** Volunteer Coordinators.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"Managing volunteers is completely different from managing paid staff. This gives you a dedicated database to track your volunteers, their availability, and their assigned projects."*

## 6. Donations
* **What it does:** The detailed ledger of every single individual donation received. It logs the donor's name, the amount, the payment method, and the project they donated to.
* **Who uses it:** Finance Team, Accounts.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is where your daily receipts live. Every time a donor gives ₹500, it is logged here, allowing you to instantly generate and send them their tax-exemption receipt."*

## 7. Reports
* **What it does:** Generates specific compliance reports required for running an NGO/Foundation (financial audits, donor statements).
* **Who uses it:** Auditors, Accountants.

## 8. Admin
* **What it does:** The backend settings specifically restricted to the UNCF module (configuring donation categories, tax settings, or project types).
* **Who uses it:** UNCF Super Admins.
