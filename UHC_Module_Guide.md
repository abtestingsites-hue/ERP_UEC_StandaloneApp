# UHC Module - Presentation Guide

The **UHC** (Universal Home/Health Care) module is the clinical core of the organization. It manages out-patient care, at-home nursing services, and tracks all clinical data (vitals, medications, health records) for the patients.

Here is the page-by-page explanation of the UHC side navigation bar:

## 1. UHC Dashboard
* **What it does:** The clinical overview. It displays critical health alerts (e.g., a patient with abnormal blood pressure), active nursing assignments, and pending medical approvals.
* **Who uses it:** Chief Medical Officers, Head Nurses.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is your digital triage board. It highlights the patients who need immediate attention across all your services, ensuring that medical emergencies are never overlooked."*

## 2. Healthcare
* **What it does:** The core medical charting module. This acts as a digital health record (EHR) where nurses log a patient's daily vitals, medication administration, and overall health progress.
* **Who uses it:** Doctors, Nurses, Medical Monitors.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This replaces physical clipboards at the patient's bedside. Your nurses enter vitals directly into the tablet, and doctors can monitor a patient's health trends remotely in real-time."*

## 3. Care Allocation
* **What it does:** The clinical roster. This is the scheduling engine used to match a specific nurse or caregiver to a specific patient for 'Home Care' (visiting a patient's house) or 'In-House Care'.
* **Who uses it:** Care Allocation Managers, Operations Team.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is how you ensure no patient is left waiting. You can visually assign a nurse's shift to a patient, preventing double-bookings and tracking exactly who is responsible for whom today."*

## 4. Medical Inventory
* **What it does:** Unlike the 'Elder Inventory' which tracks food and cleaning supplies, this is strictly for clinical inventory (medicines, syringes, oxygen tanks, first aid supplies).
* **Who uses it:** Pharmacists, Head Nurses.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"Clinical supplies are expensive and heavily regulated. This strictly tracks your medical stock, ensuring you never run out of critical medications while preventing inventory shrinkage."*
