# UA Module - Presentation Guide

The **UA** (Universal Ambulance) module is the logistics and emergency transport engine of the organization. It manages the entire ambulance fleet, driver dispatch, and emergency routing.

Here is the page-by-page explanation of the UA side navigation bar:

## 1. UA Dashboard
* **What it does:** The fleet command center. It gives a real-time overview of active field duties, emergency calls received, vehicles currently dispatched, and overall ambulance billing revenue.
* **Who uses it:** Fleet Managers, Head Dispatchers.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is your air-traffic control tower for the fleet. You can instantly see how many ambulances are out on duty, how many are available for an emergency, and how much revenue the fleet is generating today."*

## 2. Ambulance Services
* **What it does:** The active dispatch and booking system. It handles live bookings from patients, hospital transfers, and tracks the current route/status of each dispatched vehicle.
* **Who uses it:** Dispatchers, Shift Managers.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"When an emergency call comes in, this page allows your dispatchers to instantly find the nearest available driver and assign them the route, minimizing response times and saving lives."*

## 3. Ambulance Support
* **What it does:** The backend logistics for the fleet. It tracks the maintenance schedules of the vehicles (oil changes, tire rotations), fuel logs, and the specific duty hours logged by the ambulance drivers.
* **Who uses it:** Fleet Maintenance Team, Operations Managers.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"Ambulances undergo heavy wear and tear. This module tracks your maintenance schedules and fuel costs, preventing unexpected breakdowns and ensuring your fleet is always road-ready and cost-efficient."*
