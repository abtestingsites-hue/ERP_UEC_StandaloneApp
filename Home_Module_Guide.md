# Home Module - Presentation Guide

While the other tabs (UNCF, UEC, UHC) are for specific branches of the organization, the **Home** tab acts as the **Master Control Room**. It aggregates data from all branches and handles the company-wide back-office tasks (like central HR and Finance).

Here is the page-by-page explanation of the Home side navigation bar:

## 1. Home (Main Dashboard)
* **What it does:** This is the bird's-eye view of the entire Universal brand. It aggregates the KPIs from all other modules (Charity + Elder Care + Healthcare + Ambulances).
* **Who uses it:** Top-level Management, Directors, and Super Admins.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"When you log in in the morning, this screen tells you the health of your entire business. You can see total revenue, total active patients across all facilities, and critical alerts without having to click into 5 different systems."*

## 2. Finance (Cashbox)
* **What it does:** The central treasury. While specific branches have their own "Elder Finance" or "Ambulance Billing", this page manages the actual physical and digital money coming in and out of the company accounts. 
* **Who uses it:** Chief Financial Officer (CFO), Senior Accountants.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is your digital ledger. It tracks every rupee that flows through the Universal ecosystem, ensuring nothing is lost or unaccounted for."*

## 3. Patient Daily Cost
* **What it does:** A specialized financial tracking page. When a nurse uses consumables on a patient (e.g., adult diapers, specific injections, extra oxygen), it is logged here daily.
* **Who uses it:** Floor Managers, Billing Department.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This prevents revenue leakage. Instead of guessing how much cotton or medicine was used at the end of the month, your nurses log the 'daily cost' in real-time, ensuring the final invoice is 100% accurate."*

## 4. Manual Patient Billing
* **What it does:** Allows the staff to generate one-off invoices or immediate bills for a patient outside of the normal automated billing cycle.
* **Who uses it:** Billing and Accounts team.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"If a patient is discharged suddenly or requests a specific one-off service, your team can generate a professional, itemized invoice immediately from this page."*

## 5. Daily Operations
* **What it does:** This is the master scheduling and logistics hub. It handles the "Shift Roster" (assigning staff to their shifts) and cross-department operational tasks.
* **Who uses it:** Operations Managers, HR Managers.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is the engine room of the business. It ensures that every patient has a caregiver assigned, and no staff member is double-booked."*

## 6. Human Resource (HR)
* **What it does:** The central employee database. It handles recruitment (the 35-field candidate form), employee onboarding, tracking their active status, and payroll setup.
* **Who uses it:** HR Department.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"You employ a lot of nurses, drivers, and caregivers. This module tracks them from the day they apply for a job until the day they resign, managing their contracts and compliance in one place."*

## 7. Workflow Timeline
* **What it does:** An audit trail. If you look at a specific Patient or Enquiry, this page shows a timeline of everything that happened to them (e.g., 'Called at 10 AM', 'Nurse Assigned at 11 AM', 'Billed at 5 PM').
* **Who uses it:** Quality Assurance, Management, Admins.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"If there is ever a dispute or a complaint, the Workflow Timeline gives you absolute transparency. It logs exactly who did what, and at what exact time."*

## 8. Reports
* **What it does:** The central analytics hub. Generates downloadable PDF/CSV reports for financial audits, patient census, and staff attendance.
* **Who uses it:** Management, Auditors.

## 9. Admin Files
* **What it does:** A secure digital vault for company compliance documents. It tracks things like Fire Safety Certificates, Ambulance Registrations, and Trade Licenses, and warns you when they are expiring.
* **Who uses it:** Administrative Heads, Legal team.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"Never pay a late fee for an expired license again. The Admin Files section holds all your legal documents and proactively alerts you 30 days before anything expires."*
