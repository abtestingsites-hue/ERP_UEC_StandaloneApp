# UEO Module - Presentation Guide

The **UEO** (Universal Enquiry Office) module acts as the CRM (Customer Relationship Management) and front door for the entire business. It handles new leads, ongoing customer relations, communications, and physical building security.

Here is the page-by-page explanation of the UEO side navigation bar:

## 1. UEO Dashboard
* **What it does:** The front-office command center. It tracks active enquiries, incoming new leads, pending follow-ups, complaints, and visitor logs.
* **Who uses it:** Receptionists, Customer Care Managers, Security Heads.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This is the front door of your business. It ensures that no incoming lead is ever forgotten, and gives you a clear picture of how many people are walking through your physical doors or calling your phone lines."*

## 2. Enquiry Desk
* **What it does:** The primary lead generation tool. When a family calls inquiring about services (like Home Care or Ambulance), their details are logged here. It tracks the status of the lead from 'New' to 'Converted'.
* **Who uses it:** Sales Team, Receptionists.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This replaces chaotic notebooks at the reception desk. Every phone call is logged as an enquiry, and you can schedule follow-up dates to ensure your team converts that lead into a paying client."*

## 3. Customer Relations
* **What it does:** Handles ongoing relationships with existing clients. This includes logging and resolving complaints, capturing daily feedback from patients, and tracking satisfaction scores.
* **Who uses it:** Customer Success Team, Quality Assurance.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"In healthcare, reputation is everything. This module allows you to catch and resolve a family's complaint immediately before it escalates, ensuring high customer satisfaction."*

## 4. Omnichannel
* **What it does:** The central communication hub. It integrates multiple communication channels (like SMS, Email, or WhatsApp) into one unified inbox so staff can message clients directly from the ERP.
* **Who uses it:** Communications Team, Customer Care.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"Your staff no longer needs to use their personal phones to text clients. Every message sent to a family is logged centrally, maintaining a professional and secure communication history."*

## 5. Security
* **What it does:** Physical gate management. It acts as a digital visitor logbook, tracking vehicles, staff, and guests entering and leaving the premises (complete with check-in/check-out times).
* **Who uses it:** Security Guards, Facility Managers.
* **Pitch to Client:** *"This modernizes your security gate. Instead of a messy paper register, guards use a tablet to log every person or vehicle that enters, giving management absolute visibility over who is currently inside the building."*
